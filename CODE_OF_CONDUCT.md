# Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BOQLint an inclusive, respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We also pledge to protect the confidentiality of engineering workbooks, client information, commercial data, and vulnerability reports shared in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- using welcoming, professional, and inclusive language;
- respecting differing regional, industry, template, and professional contexts;
- giving specific, evidence-based feedback about code or ideas rather than people;
- accepting constructive feedback and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- helping newcomers understand technical and domain constraints;
- using entirely fictional examples in public discussions; and
- prioritizing community safety, privacy, accessibility, and the project's documented boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome advances, harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments and personal or political attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- uploading real engineering files, client information, non-public prices, credentials, or sensitive security evidence to public project spaces;
- knowingly contributing restricted standards text, proprietary databases, or third-party commercial samples without permission;
- spam, advertising, coordinated disruption, or bad-faith manipulation of issues and reviews; and
- other conduct that a reasonable professional community would consider inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-controlled communication channels, and public spaces when an individual is officially representing BOQLint.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a private contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If the report includes a vulnerability or sensitive workbook evidence, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not create a public issue.

Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Remove unrelated personal, client, project, and commercial data. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people necessary to evaluate and respond to the report. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to impact, context, repetition, and willingness to repair harm.

1. **Correction** — A private or public explanation of the issue and requested change.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning with clear expectations and consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction** — Time-limited suspension from interaction or representation.
4. **Permanent restriction** — Permanent removal for sustained harassment, threats, malicious disclosure, or repeated serious violations.

Anyone making an enforcement decision should disclose relevant conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when appropriate. Reports and enforcement details should remain confidential except where disclosure is necessary for safety or law.
